# SproutPass phone walkthrough

- `sproutpass-demo.mp4`: 40-second, 1080 × 1920 H.264/AAC portrait video for sharing, with English on-screen explanations, amplified in-app sound effects, and a quiet original synthesized backing track.
- `sproutpass-preview.gif`: compact looping preview for the repository README.
- `sproutpass-poster.jpg`: static poster.

## What the video shows

The local mock app runs the existing encounter and trade workflow with staged family profiles, inventory and confirmed needs. Actual browser frames show the encounter and celebration animations; explanatory cards and held screenshots make the steps readable. The buyer reviews a fleece bear jacket, approves the $21.60 offer, and receives a local simulated payment record. All transaction shots come from the same sample trade.

The footage is edited for pacing rather than an uninterrupted recording. The companion chat is shown as an explanatory cutaway after the encounter.

## Boundaries

Location, payment and companion protocol execution are simulated in this offline capture. No live generative AI request, Photon send or iMessage delivery is demonstrated. No Visa API, real charge, funds transfer or physical clothing handoff is involved. Those boundaries also appear on screen. The repository's live implementation and its verification instructions are documented separately.

Sound effects reuse the actual files in `public/sfx/` (matching alerts, selection, acceptance, coins and the success jingle), synchronized to the edited footage. Effects are prominently amplified over the background track; peak limiting prevents clipping.

No third-party music or stock footage is included. The pixel assets are from this repository. The video introduces no new product behavior and makes no claim of parent validation.
