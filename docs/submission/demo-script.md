# SproutPass demo

## Two-minute staged walkthrough

Say: “SproutPass helps parents pass outgrown children’s clothes to another family without managing a marketplace listing and conversation themselves. This demonstration uses sample clothing and simulated payment.”

1. Open `/trial`. Click **Prepare Sample + Need**. Show the sample in Freja’s inventory and the compatible need in Olivia’s account. Say: “These are staged inputs, saved in Base44.” The sample is a $30 romper, listed for $18.
2. Click **Create Demo Offer** to demonstrate the direct offer path. For the proximity demonstration instead, open Freja’s and Olivia’s world links on separate phones, keep both screens open and use Olivia’s walk button. Clearly say the walk is simulated.
3. Show the final garment image, condition, size and price in Olivia’s offer. Show the iMessage proposal if the phones are connected. The seller does not have to approve again.
4. Olivia clicks **Approve** or texts **approve** in her private agent thread. Show the Visa simulation receipt and the garment now in Olivia’s inventory. No real money moves.
5. Return to `/trial`. Show the saved stages and receipt submission status. Show the actual iMessage receipt and image on both phones before claiming end-to-end receipt delivery.

Repeated setup reuses the same sample. It does not reset inventories or reverse a completed trade. Do not approve on a participant’s behalf unless they have explicitly asked you to perform that demonstration action.
